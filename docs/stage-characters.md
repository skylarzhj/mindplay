# Independent stage identity

Brain Teaser is Stage 02 on the five-island map, with its own transparent racing-island artwork. The detached homepage invitation has been removed. Curious Island and Brain Teaser Island route to different games; the lucky die can choose either open world.

Orin is a violet star-robed keeper with a crystal staff and beard. Captain Rowan is a green-coated royal race marshal with a feathered cap, sash, and pennant. Their SVG artwork is native component code so eyes, mouths, arms, expressions, and props can animate independently. Game state drives `idle`, `thinking`, `anticipating`, `celebrating`, and `teasing`. Both respond to a click with a wave. Reduced-motion preferences stop ambient blinking, speaking, bobbing, and repeated gestures.

Racecourse panels and controls use sage green and warm gold; Dice Duel retains lilac and ivory. These identities share the storybook design language while keeping characters and locations distinct.

## Artwork
